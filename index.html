Before you book

Questions people actually ask

Will I look frozen or unnatural?

The short answer: not with good technique, appropriate dosing, and a provider who’s listening to what you actually want.

“Frozen” results are almost always the product of too many units, placed without attention to how your specific face moves. Peachy’s AI-guided mapping and natural-result focus exist specifically to prevent this. Our most common review phrase is “natural-looking.” That’s not a tagline — it’s what people say when describing their results.

What does “flat-fee pricing” actually mean?

It means you pay one price for your treatment — not a per-unit price that varies based on how much product your provider decides you need in the moment. Your total is visible before you book. It doesn’t change when you arrive.

The flat-fee model removes the financial guesswork that makes most people anxious about Botox pricing — and the structural upsell incentive that creates distrust at per-unit clinics.

What if I don’t like my results?

Two weeks after your treatment, Botox has fully settled. That’s your check-in window. If anything looks slightly uneven or needs a small adjustment, come back and we’ll take care of it. This is included in every treatment — not as a policy exception, but as a standard part of how Peachy operates.

I’m scared of needles. Will it hurt?

This is one of the most common things people tell us — and one of the most common things they’re surprised by afterward. Most clients describe the sensation as a quick, small pinch. Some feel almost nothing.

Your provider will explain what they’re doing in real time and move at a pace you’re comfortable with. We can’t promise it’s completely painless for everyone, but the experience is far gentler than most first-timers expect.

How long does a treatment take?

Most treatments take 15–30 minutes, including your personalized mapping. Many clients book on a lunch break. Rebooking, once you’re a member, takes about 90 seconds.
